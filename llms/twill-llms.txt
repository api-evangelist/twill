# Twill

> Twill (withtwill.com) is a referral-based recruiting platform — "talent recommended talent." It makes hiring easier and faster by having a network of 50,000+ vetted, experienced professionals (engineering, product, sales, growth) recommend their peers and friends for open roles, producing warm introductions to passive candidates instead of recruiter cold outreach. Twill advertises an average 22-day time to hire, a 90-day retention guarantee, flat-fee pricing, and a matching partnership with MIT's Generative AI Lab. Backed by Bloomberg Beta. Twill does not currently publish a public developer API, API documentation, or machine-readable specifications.
